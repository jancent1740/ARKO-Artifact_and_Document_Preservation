The system must allow Museum Staff to efficiently manage visitor bookings, attendance, and daily operations so they can maintain accurate records and ensure smooth museum workflows.	
The system shall allow staff to view, create, and modify visitor bookings for both individual and group visits.
The system shall enable staff to manually record visitor arrivals and departures, including date, time, and group information, in a secure logbook interface.
The system shall provide real-time reports on on-site visitor attendance, including total visitors, group size.
The system shall allow staff to search and filter visitor bookings and logbook entries by date, visitor name, group type, or assigned staff.
The system shall generate notifications for upcoming visits and incomplete logbook entries.
The system shall detect scheduling conflicts automatically, such as overlapping visitor bookings or double-assigned staff, and notify staff.
The system shall provide an intuitive and responsive interface suitable for daily use on desktop and tablet devices.
The system shall store historical visitor logs, staff schedules, and attendance records to support auditing and reporting.
The system shall allow staff to export attendance and scheduling data in standard formats (CSV or PDF) for reporting and analysis.
The system shall provide a dashboard summarizing the day’s bookings, staff assignments, and visitor log entries, highlighting any anomalies or missing data.
The system shall allow Museum Staff to set or change their active museum context from within the operational interface, ensuring that all logbook entries, staff assignments, attendance reports, and scheduling views reflect data exclusively for the currently selected museum until the context is explicitly changed.

The system must enable Curators to manage staff assignments, oversee visitor operations, and make operational decisions based on accurate, up-to-date data.
The system shall allow curators to assign staff or caretakers to specific galleries, exhibits, or time slots.
The system shall allow curators to upload the template for the slot availability that will be seen on the live availability of each and every museum, and update the schedule availability of whether it is open for slot, close, or unavailable because of holidays.
The system shall allow curators to approve or adjust visitor schedules and staff assignments.
The system shall provide visual indicators for conflicts or gaps in staff assignments or visitor scheduling.
The system shall allow curators to view historical attendance trends and staff activity reports.
The system shall provide notifications to curators regarding high visitor load periods or incomplete logbook entries.
The system shall allow curators to restrict or grant role-based access to staff for specific operational functions.
The system shall provide an integrated overview dashboard showing visitor activity, staff allocation, and operational alerts.
The system shall support exporting curator-level operational reports in standard formats (CSV, PDF).
The system shall allow curators to track staff performance metrics, including entries logged, schedule adherence, and attendance monitoring.
The system shall enable curators to plan recurring schedules and block special events or holidays efficiently.
The system shall allow Curators to set or change their active museum context from within the operational interface, ensuring that all logbook entries, staff assignments, attendance reports, and scheduling views reflect data exclusively for the currently selected museum until the context is explicitly changed.


The system must allow the General Public to independently discover, book, and manage their museum visits through a publicly accessible, self-service scheduling interface.
The system shall allow the general public to browse available visit schedules, including gallery openings, time slots, and remaining capacity, without requiring prior registration.
The system shall allow the general public to submit visit booking requests for individual or group visits by providing their name, contact information, group size, preferred date, and time slot.
The system shall automatically validate public booking submissions for scheduling conflicts, capacity limits, and required field completeness before confirming or rejecting the request.
The system shall generate and deliver a booking confirmation, including a reference number, visit date, time, and group details, to the visitor's provided email address upon successful submission.
The system shall allow visitors to retrieve and view their existing booking details by entering their booking reference number or registered email address through a public lookup interface.
The system shall allow visitors to cancel or request a modification to an existing booking within a system-defined cancellation window, with automatic notification sent to confirm the change.
The system shall notify visitors of booking status updates, including approval, rejection, modification, or cancellation, through their registered email address in a timely and clear manner.
The system shall display real-time capacity information for each available time slot so that visitors can make informed scheduling decisions before submitting a booking request.
The system shall allow visitors to indicate special requirements during booking submission, such as accessibility needs or group type (e.g., school group, senior group, or private tour), so that staff can prepare accordingly.
The system shall provide visitors with a publicly accessible FAQ or help section within the scheduling interface that explains the booking process, cancellation policy, and museum visit guidelines.
The system shall display an interactive, real-time availability calendar, that visually indicates for each date whether visit slots are fully available, partially available, or fully booked, enabling visitors to identify bookable dates at a glance before selecting a specific time slot.
The system shall require the user to select a specific museum from a clearly presented dropdown or selection interface before accessing any scheduling, calendar, or booking functionality, so that all subsequent scheduling operations, availability displays, capacity data, and booking submissions are scoped exclusively to the chosen museum and do not reflect or include data from any other museum in the network.
